Privacy Policy

Protecting your information matters.

Last updated: November 16, 2026

TrialGuard: Renewal Alerts ("TrialGuard") is provided by Eluma Technologies. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our subscription-tracking application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we collect:

  • Email address for account registration and login.
  • A unique account identifier used to associate your information with your account.
  • Subscription details you choose to enter, including service names, prices, billing frequencies, and renewal dates.
  • Information you choose to submit when contacting our support team.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information to:

  • Create and authenticate your account.
  • Store and display your subscriptions.
  • Calculate upcoming renewal dates and costs.
  • Provide renewal reminders.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.
  • Maintain the security and functionality of TrialGuard.

3. Screenshot Scanning

TrialGuard is designed to extract subscription details from screenshots selected by users. Our intended implementation processes screenshots on the user's device without uploading the original image to our servers. Only subscription details that users choose to save are intended to be stored in their accounts.

This feature's data handling will be verified before public release, and this policy will be updated to reflect the final implementation.

4. Data Storage and Service Providers

TrialGuard uses Supabase for account authentication and storage of subscription information. Information is transmitted to and processed by Supabase to provide these services.

We do not sell your personal information. Other service providers, including app-store payment services, may process information when their related features are enabled. We will update this policy to describe those services before their public release.

5. Notifications

TrialGuard provides reminders about upcoming subscription renewals. Notification permissions can be managed through your device settings.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain account and subscription information while it is needed to operate your account, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

You may request account and personal data deletion by emailing the address below. We are also developing an in-app account deletion feature for release.

7. Data Security

We use service-provider security features and access controls intended to protect account information. No electronic storage or transmission system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law. You can also manage notification permissions through your device settings.

9. Children's Privacy

TrialGuard is not specifically designed for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as TrialGuard develops. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
